## CIS 641 Term Project

### Vehicle & Boat Maintenance Tracker

For my Systems Analysis and Design course, I am
developing a Vehicle & Boat Maintenance Tracker.

The application will allow users to keep track of their
cars, trucks, boats, and other vehicles in one place.

Users will be able to record maintenance history,
track mileage or engine hours, store repair notes,
and schedule upcoming maintenance.

The goal is to create a simple application that helps
vehicle owners stay organized and keep up with
regular maintenance.

Throughout the project, I am also applying software
engineering practices such as requirements gathering,
UML modeling, system design, and testing.
